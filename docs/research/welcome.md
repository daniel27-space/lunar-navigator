# Welcome cards and the two views: what they borrow

Written 15 Nov 2026. Sources were read as search results and summaries, not in full.

## Welcome cards
- Cards that only explain do not make people better at an app (Nielsen Norman Group, 70 users,
  4 apps: 91% task success after reading the deck, 94% after skipping it). So the cards here say
  what the app is for, in four short cards, and the controls are taught afterwards by doing.
  https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-tutorials/
- A loading wait is the one moment cards cost nothing: the cards cover the app while the terrain
  downloads, and say when the Moon is ready. Game loading screens work the same way.
- Always a visible Skip (Apple Human Interface Guidelines, onboarding).
  https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding
- One idea per card, a headline that names what the visitor gets, progress dots (our judgement).
- Not followed: Apple and the game-design writing both say not to repeat the cards for returning
  visitors. They are shown on every load at the owner's request, because for the challenge every
  load is a new visitor. Skip is one click.

## Two views
- Ask once at the start, allow changing later: Microsoft Flight Simulator's assistance presets.
  https://www.flightsimulator.com/accessibility/
- Name each view for what you do in it, not for skill: Kerbal Space Program's Sandbox, Science
  and Career. Hence "Explorer" and "Mission planner", not "simple" and "advanced".
- Neither is the "proper" one: Celeste rewrote its Assist Mode wording for this reason. The two
  buttons are the same size and the card says the data is the same either way.
- Change what is visible, not the engine: one set of data and figures, with specialist controls
  put away in the Explorer view (progressive disclosure, Nielsen Norman Group).

What differs today: the Explorer view starts the hands-on tour and hides the height and slope
layers, coordinate entry, the assumptions, the terrain-resolution notes and export. The planner
view shows everything and starts no tour.
